# Wingman Vision

## Product Thesis

Most job-search products optimize application volume.

Wingman optimizes the quality of opportunities created.

The system should help the user understand:

- where unusually strong opportunities may exist;
- why a role is or is not a fit;
- what evidence supports that conclusion;
- who may credibly open a door;
- what the next highest-leverage action is;
- how to prepare without losing authenticity.

## What Wingman Is

Wingman is a local-first career intelligence and decision-support system.

It combines:

- a canonical professional profile;
- opportunity analysis;
- company research;
- relationship intelligence;
- action prioritization;
- drafting support;
- interview preparation.

## What Wingman Is Not

Wingman is not:

- an autonomous applicant;
- a mass-application engine;
- a generic resume keyword optimizer;
- a CRM that merely tracks status;
- a system that invents familiarity, referrals, achievements, or evidence;
- a product that sends messages without explicit approval.

## North Star

> Find a small number of exceptional opportunities and make the user the obvious candidate for the best of them.

## Product Invariants

1. Human approval before external action.
2. Evidence before assertion.
3. Local-first private data.
4. Least-privilege tools and connectors.
5. Deterministic controls for permissions and validation.
6. Inspectable outputs and provenance.
7. Provider-neutral architecture.
8. No invented familiarity.
9. Partial truth over polished fiction.
10. Usable vertical slices — every phase ends in something useful.
11. Evaluation precedes increased autonomy.

This list is mirrored verbatim in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md); change both together.

## Long-Term Outcome

Wingman becomes a continuously learning career operating system that maintains a living model of the user's work, relationships, opportunities, and market intelligence, then recommends the highest-leverage actions every day.
