What we believe

Northwind Labs builds infrastructure for teams who ship. We started because deployment tooling had become a tax on the people doing the work, and we thought that was fixable.

We optimise for the reader, not the writer

A document that takes an hour to write and saves ten people ten minutes each is a good trade. We hold written communication to a high bar precisely because it scales — and we would rather spend the hour than ship a paragraph nobody can act on.

Ship small, ship often

We do not believe in the big-bang release. Every change should be small enough that one person can hold it in their head, and reversible enough that a bad call costs an afternoon rather than a quarter.

  • Deploy on Fridays. If you can’t, fix the deploy.
  • No change is too small to review.
  • The person who wrote it owns the pager for it.

Disagreement is cheap; silence is expensive

We would rather have the argument in the room than the retrospective. People here are expected to say the uncomfortable thing early, and to change their mind in public when the evidence moves.

“Strong opinions, weakly held” is not a licence to be careless with either half.

Remote by default, deliberate in person

We are a remote-first company with quarterly on-sites. Being distributed is not a perk we offer; it is the operating model we chose, and it only works if the written record is actually the source of truth.

Who we are

We are Northwind—forty people in nine countries. Our founder’s rule: hire people who finish things1.
